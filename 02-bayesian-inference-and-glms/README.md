# Bayesian Inference & GLMs (Labs 4–6)

## Lab 4: Bayesian estimation in hierarchical models
- Built a **Beta-Binomial hierarchical model** to estimate per-group rates, and compared the naive estimate with the Bayesian posterior.
- Derived the joint, prior, likelihood and marginal distributions, and fit the model with **PyMC**.

## Lab 5: Sampling methods and GLMs
- Implemented **rejection sampling** and **Gibbs sampling** from scratch.
- Fit **generalized linear models** in both frequentist and Bayesian form.

## Lab 5.5 and Lab 6: GLMs and the bootstrap
- Modeled **Atlantic hurricane counts** with **Poisson GLMs** using **Bambi**, PyMC and statsmodels.
- Ran **posterior predictive checks** with ArviZ to compare models with and without sea-surface temperature as a predictor.
- Used **kernel density estimation** and the **bootstrap** to test whether a galaxy dataset has more than one mode.

## Skills
Bayesian inference, hierarchical models, MCMC (PyMC), rejection and Gibbs sampling, Poisson GLMs, posterior predictive checks, bootstrap hypothesis testing, kernel density estimation, Bambi, ArviZ, statsmodels.
